# ⚡ AI Unleashed — ICI Fest 2026

> **Challenge the Intelligence.**
>
> An immersive, interactive event website created for **AI Unleashed**, a 3-round AI challenge presented as part of **ICI Fest 2026** at **Swami Keshvanand Institute of Technology, Management & Gramothan (SKIT), Jaipur**.

---

## 🌌 About the Project

**AI Unleashed** is an interactive event experience designed to introduce participants to a competition where human problem-solving meets artificial intelligence.

Rather than functioning as a traditional event webpage, the site presents the competition as a visual journey through:

- The Challenge
- The Journey
- Round 01 — AI Quiz
- Round 02 — Head-to-Head Solutions
- Round 03 — Guess the Prompt
- The Final
- Event Coordinates
- Registration

The website is designed around a cinematic, futuristic interface with smooth scrolling, animated transitions, ambient sound, interactive elements, and stage-based navigation.
